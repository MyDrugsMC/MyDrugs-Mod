# MyDrugs — Vision

MyDrugs is a survival/progression mod about substances, altered states, addiction, recovery, and integration.

It is **not** a moral-panic mod and it is **not** a pure horror mod. The player can have fun with substances, industrialize them, become tempted by power and shortcuts, and then discover that real freedom comes from recovery, reflection, healthier habits, and psychedelic-assisted integration.

The central question is:

> Am I using this to live more freely, or am I using it to avoid myself?

## Core fantasy

The player discovers drugs as tools: energy, focus, calm, danger, productivity, rituals, machines, and altered perception.

At first, drugs are exciting. The player learns to grow, process, refine, automate, mix, and optimize them. The early and mid game should feel playful and powerful.

Then the mod slowly shows the cost of relying on the strongest shortcuts: addiction, withdrawal, crashes, body strain, stress, and psychological fragmentation.

The positive turn comes through recovery systems and psychedelics. LSD and mushrooms are not just stronger drugs; they are non-addictive altered-state keys that can open insight, inner-world exploration, and healing when combined with preparation, safe setting, diary work, therapy-like recovery, and integration.

The end of the mod should feel positive:

> I did not escape the pain. I learned how to carry it, understand it, and live freely again.

## Design pillars

1. **Substances are tools, not collectibles.** Every substance needs a clear gameplay identity and a reason to exist.
2. **Escape and integration are different.** Some routes create productivity, power, or avoidance. Recovery and psychedelics should help transform the loop instead of simply adding stronger buffs.
3. **Addiction is a system, not a punishment.** It should be readable, recoverable, and avoidable with careful play. The mod should tempt the player, not railroad them into suffering.
4. **Psychedelics are special.** LSD and mushrooms should focus on insight, perception, healing, inner-world access, and integration. They should not be balanced like ordinary addictive power drugs.
5. **Recovery is central.** Therapy-like activities, diary work, exercise, diet, sleep, music, safe rooms, and sober time should become real progression tools.
6. **The diary gives meaning.** Machines and the guide tell the player how to progress. The diary tells the player what that progress is doing to them.
7. **Endgame means freedom.** Completing the mod should reduce chores and open play, not trap the player in permanent maintenance.
8. **The mod must be responsible.** Avoid real-world synthesis instructions, dosage claims, medical claims, or glamorization. Use fictionalized, gameplay-first, 18+ framing.

## Story arc

```text
Discovery
→ control
→ temptation
→ dependence risk
→ psychedelic opening
→ recovery and integration
→ inner-world dimension
→ confrontation with the loop
→ freedom
```

## First playable target

The first strong playable version should prove this loop:

```text
Find plants
→ process them with primitive tools
→ consume or ritualize them
→ gain useful effects with visible consequences
→ build the first machines
→ automate and optimize
→ experience dose/addiction pressure if overused
→ use the diary and recovery tools to stabilize
→ use LSD/mushrooms to unlock insight systems
→ begin the integration path
```

## Tone

- Strange, bodily, mystical, vulnerable, and Minecraft-like.
- Fun and power are allowed. The player should understand why people would chase the effects.
- The mod should become more reflective over time, not more cruel.
- Bad trips and inner entities are psychological-symbolic challenges, not “drugs are evil” monsters.
- The dimension should be beautiful, surreal, and emotionally intense before it is dangerous.
- Humor is fine, but recovery and addiction should not be mocked.

## What to avoid

- A final message that says only “don’t do drugs.”
- Forced addiction as mandatory progression.
- Constant endgame chores.
- A needy Psychotrope machine that monopolizes the mod.
- Psychedelics as magic cures with no preparation or integration.
- Real-world synthesis procedures or medical instructions.
- Endless machine chains with no emotional or gameplay decision.
- Random punishments that feel like bugs.
