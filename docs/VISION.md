# Vision

`mydrugs` is a Minecraft survival mod about temptation, altered perception, consequence, recovery, and integration.

The fantasy is not “realistic drug manufacturing.” The fantasy is: strange substances and rituals can open power, perception, and machinery that vanilla Minecraft cannot, but the player must learn the difference between escape, overclocking, and healing.

## Pillars

1. **Risk/reward survival** — substances and machines create real utility with readable risks.
2. **Server-authoritative systems** — gameplay truth lives on the server.
3. **Fictional abstraction** — recipes and rituals are Minecraft-like, symbolic, and non-instructional.
4. **Readable feedback** — effects should be felt and understood.
5. **Recovery matters** — addiction/recovery is a playable arc, not a moralizing punishment.
6. **Beauty and strangeness** — psychedelic content should be beautiful, uncanny, and meaningful.
7. **Integration over domination** — the positive ending should make the player freer, not just stronger.

## Tone

- Strange, ritual, surreal, sometimes dangerous.
- Serious about consequences without moral panic.
- Curious and reflective rather than edgy for shock value.
- More “inner landscape and dangerous power” than “crime simulator.”

## Anti-goals

- Real-world synthesis, preparation, extraction, or dosing instructions.
- Medical advice or treatment claims.
- Forced addiction as a mandatory progression step.
- Unreadable random punishment.
- Client-authoritative gameplay.
- Huge context-heavy docs that agents cannot follow.

## Emotional palette

- Temptation.
- Unease.
- Power with consequences.
- Ritual certainty.
- Industrial occultism.
- Fragile recovery.
- Strange discovery.
- Survival pressure.

## What the mod should never feel like

- A meme drug mod.
- A real-world drug tutorial.
- A generic tech mod.
- A pure power fantasy without consequence.
- A punishment simulator without agency.
- A medical simulator.

## Fantasy by pillar

- **Power fantasy** — power should feel tempting, useful, and risky.
- **Horror fantasy** — horror should come from instability, mutation, perception shifts, dependence, withdrawal, and the feeling that power has a cost.
- **Recovery fantasy** — recovery should feel like a meaningful arc, not a simple stat reset.
- **Ritual fantasy** — rituals should feel symbolic, strange, and gameplay-focused. They must not provide real-world procedural chemistry or drug preparation.
- **Machine fantasy** — machines should feel dangerous, occult-industrial, and connected to the mod identity.
- **Exploration fantasy** — exploration should feel strange, risky, and rewarding.

## Humor boundaries

Humor may exist, but addiction, withdrawal, overdose-like risk, and recovery should not be treated as throwaway jokes.

## Realism boundaries

Use abstraction and fictionalization. Avoid real-world synthesis, preparation, extraction, purification, dosing, or optimization.

## Accessibility boundaries

Respect client accessibility toggles such as reduced motion. Reduced motion should reduce aggressive visual motion without disabling gameplay.

## Drug identities and feedback

Canonical drug identities and risk language, and the effect-feedback readability rule, live in `docs/GAMEPLAY_DESIGN.md`. Do not restate them here.
