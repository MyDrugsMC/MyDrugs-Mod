# Vision

`mydrugs` is a Minecraft survival mod about temptation, altered perception, consequence, recovery, and integration.

The fantasy is not “realistic drug manufacturing.” The fantasy is: strange substances and rituals can open power, perception, and machinery that vanilla Minecraft cannot, but the player must learn the difference between escape, overclocking, and healing.

## Pillars

1. **Risk/reward survival** — substances and machines create real utility with readable risks.
2. **Server-authoritative systems** — gameplay truth lives on the server.
3. **Fictional abstraction** — recipes and rituals are Minecraft-like, symbolic, and non-instructional.
4. **Readable feedback** — effects should be felt and understood.
5. **Recovery matters** — addiction/recovery is a playable arc, not a moralizing punishment.
6. **Beauty and strangeness** — psychedelic content should be beautiful, uncanny, and meaningful.
7. **Integration over domination** — the positive ending should make the player freer, not just stronger.

## Tone

- Strange, ritual, surreal, sometimes dangerous.
- Serious about consequences without moral panic.
- Curious and reflective rather than edgy for shock value.
- More “inner landscape and dangerous power” than “crime simulator.”

## Anti-goals

- Real-world synthesis, preparation, extraction, or dosing instructions.
- Medical advice or treatment claims.
- Forced addiction as a mandatory progression step.
- Unreadable random punishment.
- Client-authoritative gameplay.
- Huge context-heavy docs that agents cannot follow.
