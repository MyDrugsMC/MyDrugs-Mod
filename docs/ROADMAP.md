# Roadmap

This is a direction map, not a promise that every item is current code.

## Milestone 0 — stability baseline

Goal: keep the project buildable and agent-safe.

- Maintain `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, and this docs folder.
- Keep Gradle wrapper and validation tasks working.
- Keep registry IDs stable.
- Keep dedicated-server safety checks clean.
- Keep guide/resource validation meaningful.

## Milestone 1 — alpha gameplay spine

Goal: make early progression readable and fun.

- Coffee as early productivity.
- Tobacco as steadiness/precision.
- Cannabis/hash as calm/stability.
- First useful ritual-drug recipes.
- Clear guide/tooltip/HUD feedback.
- Avoid forcing addiction for progression.

## Milestone 2 — machine clarity

Goal: make machine chains understandable and maintainable.

- Keep machine descriptors accurate.
- Ensure menus/screens show missing requirements/status.
- Ensure recipe displays/JEI are useful.
- Keep generated resources in sync.

## Milestone 3 — risk and addiction polish

Goal: make consequence readable and recoverable.

- Tune addiction/tolerance/withdrawal after playtesting.
- Improve stimulant crash readability.
- Keep bad trips meaningful but recoverable.
- Ensure recovery paths are visible.

## Milestone 4 — recovery and diary update

Goal: make recovery a first-class progression path.

- Expand sanctuary feedback.
- Make diary blockers actionable.
- Add recovery milestones and rewards.
- Tie integration requirements to safe context and spacing.

## Milestone 5 — Psychotrope Resonator / integration machinery

Goal: make late machinery support perception, dimension access, and recovery.

- Favor deterministic states over random punishment.
- Use Dream/Integration states for dimension and recovery materials.
- Keep it ritual-technical, not real-world chemical procedure.

## Milestone 6 — Inner dimension

Goal: make the dimension beautiful, symbolic, useful, and configurable.

- Safe first-entry zone.
- Symbolic regions/biomes.
- Integration materials.
- Accessibility-friendly visuals.

## Milestone 7 — body/mutation review

Goal: align mutation/body progression with recovery and integration.

- Prefer resilience/regulation/adaptation over pure stat inflation.
- Keep old registry IDs if needed; change player-facing text first.
- Make body optimization a questioned branch, not the final answer.

## Milestone 8 — positive ending

Goal: end with integration and freedom.

- Boss symbolizes loop/false cure/craving/unintegrated self.
- Rewards reduce chores and increase freedom.
- Post-boss dimension/world state becomes calmer and safer.
