# Recovery, Diary, and Integration

Recovery is not a punishment layer. It is a progression loop about regulation, safety, reflection, and turning dangerous power into stable freedom.

## Packages

- `recovery/*` — recovery blocks/items and sanctuary systems.
- `diary/*` — diary state, snapshots, blockers, entries, player reflection.
- `core/drug/integration/*` — integration tiers, requirements, traits, recovery progress.
- `addiction/manager/recovery/*` — addiction-side recovery modifiers.
- `client/recovery/*`, `client/diary/*` — presentation, screens, disclaimers, music/tools.

## Design goals

- Make recovery visible and rewarding.
- Let addiction be avoidable and recoverable.
- Make diary entries useful, not decorative.
- Make psychedelics about perception/integration, not generic stat boosts.
- Let late-game recovery reduce chores and increase freedom.

## Sanctuary principles

A recovery sanctuary should communicate safety through block/module detection and readable feedback. It should not become a hidden checklist with no explanation.

Good rewards:

- reduced withdrawal severity;
- clearer diary guidance;
- safer integration windows;
- stronger resilience;
- better recovery action efficiency.

Bad rewards:

- mandatory chores;
- random sabotage;
- medical claims;
- real-world treatment instructions.

## Diary principles

The diary should:

- explain current blockers;
- hint next steps without spoiling everything;
- separate guide facts from personal reflection;
- track recovery/integration milestones;
- react to repeated escape/overclock loops;
- support the story arc from discovery to freedom.

## Integration principles

Integration converts risky insight into stable capabilities. It should require safety, spacing, clean streaks, or recovery context where appropriate.

Do not let integration become a bypass for all consequences. Do not make it a new addiction loop.

## Client disclaimer/external tools

Recovery music/external-tool features have extra safety constraints. Preserve disclaimer screens and SHA-256-verified downloader behavior. Do not remove external-tool safety checks to simplify code.
