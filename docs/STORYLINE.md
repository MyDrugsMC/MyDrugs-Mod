# Storyline Spine

Use this as tone guidance for content, diary, guide hints, dimension design, and endgame planning.

## Arc

```text
Discovery → Control → Temptation → Consequence → Recovery → Integration → Freedom
```

## Phase meanings

### Discovery

The player finds materials and strange mechanics. The world feels larger than vanilla, but not yet hostile.

### Control

Machines, substances, and rituals let the player accelerate work, unlock perception, and build new tools.

### Temptation

Overclocking becomes attractive. The player can get more output, faster, with increasing instability.

### Consequence

Addiction, withdrawal, bad trips, crashes, or instability become visible. Consequences should be readable and recoverable.

### Recovery

The player builds safety, uses diary/reflection systems, and learns that recovery is an active progression path.

### Integration

Insight becomes stable power. Psychedelic/recovery systems turn temporary perception into durable freedom.

### Freedom

The endgame should reduce dependency on loops of escape and overclocking. Rewards should make the player freer, not trapped in new chores.

## Boss/endgame direction

The final opponent should symbolize the loop, false cure, craving, hollow protector, or unintegrated self. The final phase should emphasize transformation or integration, not only killing.

## Writing rules

- Keep text in-world and metaphorical.
- Avoid medical claims.
- Avoid real-world procedural details.
- Let the diary sound personal; let the guide sound practical.
